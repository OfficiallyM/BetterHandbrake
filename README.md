# Better handbrake

## Features

### Tap to toggle, hold to pull
- **Tap** the handbrake key to latch it on or off, exactly like vanilla.
- **Hold** the key and the handbrake ramps up progressively, taking about a second to reach full. Let go and it releases.
- A latched handbrake stays on when you leave the car, so parking works as normal.

### Slide
Pulling the handbrake at speed reduces sideways grip on the handbrake wheels, so the rear of the car slides out instead of the car just slowing down.
